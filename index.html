Do I need to jailbreak my console?

No. Prospero-Multi-Tools only manages backup files on your computer. It does not interact with your console directly or require any modification to your PlayStation devices.

Can I organize backups from different consoles together?

Absolutely. The software separates backups by console type automatically, making it easy to find what you need regardless of whether it is a PS2 game or a PS5 title.
